# Concession Sales Analytics — Bismarck Larks

Analyzed 16,755 concession transactions from the 2026 season during my Ballpark Operations Internship with the Bismarck Larks.

### What I Analyzed

* Time Analysis — Identified peak demand periods for hot food and beer.
* Market Basket Analysis — Identified frequently purchased products and cross-selling opportunities.

### Key Findings

* Hot food sales peaked between 7:00–8:00 PM, with 45.73% of total sales occurring during these two periods.
* Selected popular beer sales remained strong from 7:30–9:30 PM, accounting for 67.83% of sales.
* Burgers and Hot Dogs had a 2.89 lift, indicating a strong purchasing association.
* Hot Dogs and Non-Alcoholic Beverages had 6.48% support and a 1.83 lift, suggesting a potential combo opportunity.
* Customers who purchased Nachos had a 52% likelihood of also purchasing a Non-Alcoholic Beverage.
